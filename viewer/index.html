About these numbers

Each pixel is radar backscatter in decibels (dB): NISAR L-band (GCOV products) or Sentinel-1 C-band (terrain-corrected RTC images), depending on the sensor selected, averaged over several looks to tame speckle. Dark means smooth surfaces such as calm water. Bright means rough ground, steep slopes, forest or buildings. A change between two passes is only meaningful for the same sensor, track and direction; the viewer never mixes them. Current NISAR public products are provisional and still being validated; treat results as preliminary.
